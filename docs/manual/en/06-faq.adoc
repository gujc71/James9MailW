[[signing-in-and-your-account]]
== Signing in and your account

**Q. I forgot my password.**
Ask your administrator to reset it. After signing in with the new password, change it in xref:05-settings.adoc#change-password[Profile].

**Q. It says I cannot sign in for a while.**
After several wrong passwords, signing in is blocked for a few minutes. Try again later.

**Q. I was suddenly signed out.**

* Changing your password elsewhere signs out all other sessions.
* Sessions expire after a certain time. Sign in again.
* Your administrator may have suspended your account.

**Q. The screen is in Korean.**
Sign out, choose **English** on the sign-in screen and sign in again.

[[receiving-mail]]
== Receiving mail

**Q. New mail does not show up right away.**
Webmail checks for new mail every 30 seconds. To see it immediately, click the mailbox again or refresh with F5.

**Q. I do not get new-mail notifications.**

* Check that 🔔 in the top bar is on.
* If the browser is blocking notifications, click the padlock to the left of the address bar and set notifications to **Allow**.
* No notification appears while you are looking at the webmail window.
* Mail that lands in Sent, Drafts, Trash or Spam is not announced. Mail filed into another mailbox by a xref:05-settings.adoc#_55-filters[filter] is announced.

**Q. Pictures in a message are missing.**
Outside pictures are blocked to prevent tracking. If you trust the message, click **Show images** (xref:02-mail-read.adoc#blocked-remote-images[2.6]).

**Q. A message I need is in Spam.**
Open it and click **Not spam**. It goes back to the Inbox and the sender is removed from your blocked senders.

**Q. Mail from a particular person never arrives.**
Check whether it is caught by a xref:05-settings.adoc#_57-blocked-senders[blocked senders], xref:05-settings.adoc#_55-filters[filter] or xref:05-settings.adoc#_58-forwarding[forwarding] rule (with Keep a copy turned off).

[[sending-mail]]
== Sending mail

**Q. It says the attachments are too large.**
Normal attachments are limited to 30MB and 10 files in total by default. Use **Convert** next to an attachment, or **Convert all to large attachments**. Large attachments can be downloaded for 30 days.

**Q. The message I was writing disappeared.**
Open a new message with **✎** in the same browser and you will see "You have an unfinished message from …". Click **Continue writing**. Attachments are not restored (xref:03-mail-compose.adoc#_38-auto-save-and-continuing-a-message[3.8]).

**Q. I want to take back a message I sent.**
If the recipients are on the same mail server and have not read it yet, you can **Recall** it under xref:03-mail-compose.adoc#_39-read-receipts-and-recall[Read receipts]. Mail sent to outside addresses cannot be recalled.

**Q. I cannot tell whether outside recipients read my mail.**
Outside recipients show as "Not trackable (external)". Tick **Request read receipt** when sending; you get a receipt if the recipient agrees.

**Q. It says "Too many attempts".**
Sending more than 60 messages a minute blocks sending for a short while. Try again after the time shown.

[[storage]]
== Storage

**Q. My mailbox is full.**

* Check the usage at the bottom of the side menu.
* See the size of each mailbox in xref:05-settings.adoc#_54-mailboxes[Mailboxes], delete large messages, then **Empty** the **Trash** (mail left in Trash still counts).
* Ask your administrator if you need more space.

[[calendar]]
== Calendar

**Q. An event I changed in Thunderbird does not show in webmail (or the other way round).**
Thunderbird synchronizes every 30 minutes by default. Synchronize in Thunderbird, or refresh the webmail calendar.

**Q. I cannot change an event.**
Events in read-only calendars (including network subscriptions) cannot be changed.

[[thunderbird-and-outlook]]
== Thunderbird and Outlook

**Q. Mail I deleted in my mail app is gone from webmail too.**
Webmail and the mail app share the same mailbox. Deleting on one side deletes on the other.

**Q. After changing my password, my mail app no longer receives mail.**
Update the password saved in your mail app (and in any app connected with CalDAV or CardDAV).

**Q. I do not know the server settings for my mail app.**
Ask your administrator for the IMAP and SMTP server addresses and ports. Calendar and address book connection details can be copied from the xref:04-calendar.adoc#connecting-other-calendar-apps-caldav[CalDAV guide] and the xref:05-settings.adoc#connecting-other-address-book-apps-carddav[CardDAV guide] in webmail.
